Layer 3 · Mechanism ladder

The science stack they lean on

MarsMen doesn't argue “more testosterone.” It argues a 4-part system: make it, keep it, protect it, use it — each with a named villain and a named ingredient.

The TRT shutdown

Villain: the needle. “Once you inject, your body forgets how to make testosterone” — the HPTA axis goes dormant (thermostat / space-heater / furnace analogy). MarsMen restarts the factory instead of replacing it.

The SHBG trap

Villain: bound testosterone. “Money in the bank but your debit card doesn't work.” Boron frees locked T so what you already make becomes usable.

Estrogen conversion

Villain: aromatase. As T drops, estrogen rises and routes fat to the chest & gut (moobs, dad bod). Tongkat Ali blocks the enzyme; Boron drops excess estrogen.

Cortisol theft

Villain: stress hormone. High cortisol steals T, bloats the face, wrecks sleep. Restore T → cortisol falls → the body “stops working against you.”

Clinical dosing as proof

Villain: the competitor. 1,000mg Tongkat / 400mg Shilajit “not 50mg,” third-party tested, made in USA, no proprietary blends — dosing is the credibility engine.
