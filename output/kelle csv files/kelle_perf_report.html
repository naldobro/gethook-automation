03 The real driver isn't the hook — it's the click quality (CVR)

Hook rate is table stakes. Almost every video clears 40–56% (3-sec/impressions). It does not predict ROAS: the best-retaining ad in the account, B#2.9 (55% hook, holds to 6% completion), still loses at 0.90.

High CTR can be a trap. B#79 has the highest link-CTR (4.94%) but 0.88 ROAS — curiosity/outrage clicks that don't buy.

CVR (purchase ÷ link click) is the separator. Winners convert 3.3–4.6% of clicks; losers 1.2–2.0%.

B#55 4.6% · CR#7 3.3% · B#88 3.3%  vs  B#28 1.2% · B#27.1 1.4% · B#79 1.5%. Angle quality shows up at the checkout, not the thumbnail.
