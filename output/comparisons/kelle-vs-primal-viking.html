The one-line verdict

Both brands run the same "root-cause reframe" engine — "it's not the hair, it's the hormone (DHT)" mirrors "it's not the fat, it's the hormone (estrogen/low-T)." Where Primal Viking pulls ahead is at the top of the funnel: a famous face, filmed ingredient provenance, a quiz, and relationship-outcome storytelling. Where Kelle pulls ahead is segmentation & objection-handling: 15 sub-avatars and dedicated rebuttal ads.

What they run BETTER than us — ranked by their impression weight

These are the plays Primal Viking leans on — several sit in their highest-impression ads — that Kelle either doesn't run or barely touches. This is the steal-list.

1

A famous / named authority face — at the very top

Their #1-impression ads (PV#1,3,4,7) are the World's Strongest Man, plus whitelisted athletes (Rampage Jackson, Dustin Poirier, Showtime Pettis). Kelle's authority is anonymous aestheticians/dermatologists. A named expert, celebrity esthetician, or influencer face is total whitespace — and it's their single biggest edge.PV#1 · top of deck

2

Ingredient provenance, filmed on-location

They show the reindeer roaming Røros, Norway — "coldest city in Europe, −56°F," "reindeer eat mushroom." Kelle claims "ancient Egyptian botanical" but never shows it. A sourcing/origin film is untested and highly credible.PV#21 · 55 · 95 · 96

3

Spouse-relationship outcomes as a core, recurring angle

Wife-POV "new husband / sex like our 20s / saved the relationship" runs all through their deck (PV#16,23,28,30,66,102). Kelle touches it once (post-divorce dating). Systematized partner-outcome testimonials are a proven Primal Viking staple we underuse.recurring high in deck

4

A quiz / diagnostic funnel

"Almost half a million men over 35 took this quiz… 60 seconds… personal 6-week reset." Kelle has no interactive lead-gen or diagnostic entry point.PV#93

5

High-drama narrative storytelling

Infidelity ("she was with another man"), the "doctor off-the-record secret." Kelle's emotional ads (mother-31-years, "that old woman") are softer — Primal Viking pushes higher-stakes drama.PV#98 · 100

6

Aggressive offer & urgency testing

60–80% off, "first 500 people," 24-hr flash, and the BOGO overstock excuse ("we made too much"). Kelle's offers are milder and guarantee-led.PV#1 · 13 · 58

7

Identity / status angle

"The most expensive thing should be your standards" — CEO, G-Wagon, first-class. Kelle is 100% problem-relief with no aspirational-identity play.PV#119

8

Problem-aggregation (many doors, one room)

They open with man boobs, beer belly, joints, ED, fertility, gut — each a separate hook routing to one root cause & product. Kelle runs mostly one problem (facial hair) × many avatars.portfolio strategy

Trends — what Primal Viking is testing right now

Reading their deck by impression priority + creation order reveals the direction of their testing.

Proven core (top of deck)

Celebrity endorsement + before/after transformation + founder story. Their highest-impression ads are the safest, most-scaled plays.

Sophistication ladder (newer ads)

Moving from simple transformations → quiz funnels, doctor-secret, infidelity drama, "not your fault" reframe, interview format. The same mechanism-depth climb Kelle went through.

Problem diversification

Newest tests expand beyond core testosterone into joints, fertility/sperm, gut health — widening the top-of-funnel net across concerns.

Volume & offer play

~40% of their library is cheap static / hook-only variants running alongside the VSLs, plus many discount tiers + BOGO + quiz A/B'd at once. High-volume, high-offer-variance testing.

The whitespace — what Kelle should test next

Four highest-leverage tests, straight from their winners

Each is proven for Primal Viking, present in their high-impression ads, and absent (or barely touched) in Kelle's 121-ad library:

  • 1 · A named authority / celebrity face — a real named dermatologist, a recognizable esthetician, or a menopause-space influencer, fronting the mechanism (their #1 lever).
  • 2 · On-location ingredient-provenance film — show the Cyperus Rotundus / Egyptian sourcing story visually, the way they film Røros.
  • 3 · A quiz funnel — "60-second facial-hair / hormone quiz → your personal plan," as an interactive top-of-funnel entry.
  • 4 · Systematized partner-outcome testimonials — the relationship/intimacy payoff (turning the cheek, being seen) as a recurring format, not a one-off.
