The strategic thesis

Smooche runs one engine across all 193 ads: reframe foundation as Korean pharmacy-grade skin science, not makeup. The enemy is always the “rigid pigment” luxury foundation ($2,000 of Chanel/Dior) that sits on top and ages you; the mechanism is always an adaptive micro-pigment that reads your skin and disappears; the proof is always someone saying “you look like sisters” or “have you had work done?”; and the close is almost always a 60%-off overstock-apology BOGO wrapped in a confession (“because I lied — we overproduced”).

Layer 3 · Mechanism ladder

Why it “reads your skin”

Smooche doesn't argue “better color.” It argues a skincare mechanism — a foundation that behaves like biology, against a rival that behaves like paint.

Adaptive micro-pigments

The magic trick. Micro-encapsulated pigments react to your skin's warmth and undertones — “goes on white and turns into your exact shade.” Solves the never-right-shade problem.

Bonds instead of sits

The enemy contrast. “Smooch bonds to skin instead of sitting on it” — no primer needed; rigid luxury pigments “sit on top, look heavy, age you.”

251% faster penetration

The number. A protein complex that penetrates “251% faster than regular foundations” — the quantified science hook.

Skincare, not makeup

The category re-frame. Hyaluronic acid keeps skin hydrated and dewy — it treats while it covers, so “the more makeup you wear” doesn't age you.

Whitespace

What they're not testing

Gaps in the current library — angles a competitor (or Smooche itself) could open up.

Younger avatar (25–35)

Almost everything targets 39–63 / menopausal skin. The “effortless everyday” frame for younger buyers is untested.

Dermatologist / clinical authority

“Korean pharmacists” carries the authority; no ad uses a named derm or clinical-study framing.

Routine-replacement math

Anti-luxury exists, but no ad hard-quantifies “replaces primer + foundation + concealer + serum.”

Subscription / refill lifecycle

Offers are all one-time BOGO; no recurring / auto-replenish angle in the set.
