The strategic thesis

Clean Nutra isn't a product brand — it's a creative-template machine. A small set of formats (ingredient personification, enemy personification, “fake vs official,” doctor dialogue, GLP-1 muscle-defense) get reskinned across seven SKUs. Every product is framed the same way: an all-in-one that replaces “$100 of separate supplements,” proven by GMP-certified / third-party-tested / made-in-USA trust cues, and closed on a BOGO + “if you still see the orange cart” scarcity. The winner right now is the Longevity Duo (NMN + Urolithin A), which alone accounts for roughly a third of the library.

Layer 3 · Mechanism ladder

The two arguments that carry the portfolio

Most SKUs lean on one of two mechanism stories.

The “two pathways” aging story

Longevity Duo. NAD “drops 50% by middle age” (the fuel) and mitochondria accumulate damage (the cleanup). “NMN restores NAD, Urolithin A clears the damaged mitochondria” — “premium gas in a dirty engine” if you only take one.

The creatine reframe

Shelf Builder. “Creatine isn't just for bodybuilders” — “lean muscle is the fastest way to burn fat,” and “creatine pulls water into the muscle cell, not under your skin” for fuller glutes.

The GLP-1 depletion hook

Cross-sell bridge. “You're losing up to 40% lean muscle… it raids the glutes first” — turns the Ozempic wave into demand for the creatine stack.

The blood-sugar / metformin angle

Glucotone. “Berberine compared directly to metformin” — insulin resistance reframed as the root of cravings, crashes, PCOS.

Whitespace

What they're not testing

Gaps across the portfolio.

Real customer transformations

Almost everything is UGC talking-head or personification; few before/after or timeline-proof formats (only one week-by-week).

Male buyer beyond VascuGlo

Men appear only for circulation. Creatine/longevity are pitched female-only despite broad appeal.

Subscription / bundle-the-portfolio

All single-SKU BOGO; no “stack Shelf Builder + Longevity” or auto-replenish cross-sell.

Authority / clinical proof

Trust rests on GMP/third-party badges; almost no named experts, studies, or dosing transparency in-ad.
